# Darwin IT Mate — Content and Trust Audit

## Step health

1. Homepage — strong structure, real business identity still needed.
2. Work — visually strong, case-study truth status must be clarified.
3. Pricing — clear hierarchy, service scope and price conditions are incomplete.
4. Contact — usable form, real contact channels and form delivery are not connected.

## Information required from the owner

### Brand and business identity

- Final business name and preferred capitalization.
- Owner/founder name and role.
- One-sentence business description.
- Short founder story and relevant experience.
- ABN and registered business name, if applicable.
- Real portrait or approved business photography.

### Contact and service area

- Business email.
- Phone number.
- Preferred contact channels: phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Messenger, email.
- Business hours.
- Areas served: Darwin, Palmerston, rural area, remote support, travel limits.
- Typical response time.
- Whether on-site, remote and after-hours support are available.

### Web design offer

- Exact inclusions for the advertised $150 website.
- Number of pages or sections.
- Domain and hosting inclusion or exclusions.
- Number of revisions.
- Copywriting and image responsibilities.
- Expected delivery time.
- Ongoing maintenance and update pricing.
- Add-ons: extra pages, bookings, e-commerce, multilingual content, SEO, analytics.
- Deposit, payment timing, cancellation and refund terms.

### IT support offer

- Supported devices, operating systems and software.
- Residential versus business support.
- Remote and on-site pricing model.
- Minimum call-out and travel charges.
- Data backup/recovery limitations.
- Services not offered.
- Privacy and data handling approach.

### Proof and credibility

- Real testimonials with permission.
- Google Business Profile URL and review rating.
- Real client logos and screenshots.
- Which portfolio projects are real, concept work or sample designs.
- Certifications, insurance, years of experience and partner status.

### Legal and operational

- Privacy policy.
- Website terms.
- Service terms and liability limitations.
- Cookie/analytics consent requirements.
- Form destination: email, CRM or booking system.
- Domain name and hosting provider.
- Social accounts.

## Placeholder content currently in the website

- `hello@darwinitmate.com.au`
- “August availability”
- “Usually the same business day”
- Kylie H. / Saltwater Building Co. testimonial
- Case-study performance metrics
- All enquiry forms currently show a local success state but do not deliver externally.

## Priority order

1. Business name, owner name, contact details and service area.
2. Exact $150 inclusions and IT support pricing rules.
3. Real proof: testimonials, Google profile and portfolio status.
4. Form delivery, legal pages and production domain.
